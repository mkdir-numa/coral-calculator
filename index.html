Coral Tribe runs an impact fund that grows through its investments and community sales revenue, with the community voting on how new capital is deployed.

Each month the fund's returns are split three ways:

  • 45% — reinvested to grow the fund
  • 40% — paid to holders who lock points in the gifts pool
  • 15% — community fund management fee

Right now the fund earns about $2,705 per month; holders receive $1,082 and management $406. These move with fund performance.

Returns depend on ReFi Hub's asset performance. Nothing is guaranteed.
